Projects

  • Go · Protobuf

    Retina — Distributed Internet Measurement Platform

    An open-source system that continuously maps Internet routes from vantage points around the world, over IPv4 and IPv6. A generator produces probing directives, the orchestrator schedules them to agents over Protobuf on TCP, and an API streams the results live to any number of subscribers. I co-designed the system and built its adaptive-rate scheduler, which paces millions of probes.

  • MLX

    Gemma 4 Inference Engine

    A local inference engine on the MLX backend, running a Q4-quantized Gemma 4 E4B model at about 26 tokens/s on an Apple silicon laptop.

  • Go · ClickHouse

    MPAT — Measurement Platform Analysis Tool

    A pipeline processing 45 TB of traceroute data in under 6 hours, while shrinking the codebase from 11,000+ to 3,000 lines. Presented at IMC ’25.
